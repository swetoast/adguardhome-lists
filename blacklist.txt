# Regexp Blacklist
### Rules are anchored /^...$/ with every literal dot escaped
### Alternations sorted alphabetically, no .* or \w
### AdGuard Home uses RE2: no lookaheads, no backreferences
### Whole-domain blocks use /^([a-z0-9-]+\.)*domain\.tld$/ to cover the domain and every subdomain
### The allowlist wins over this list, so anything here that is also in whitelist.txt is not blocked
### Layout: # type, ## vendor, ### comment

# Patterns

## Telemetry Keywords
### Matches the keyword only as its own subdomain label, never inside a site name
### Blocks metrics.icloud.com and telemetry.example.net, leaves dtrace.org and marketingland.com alone
### "tracking" and "tracker" are left out on purpose: parcel tracking and torrent trackers use them

/^([a-z0-9-]+\.)*([a-z0-9]+-)?(analytics|annons|banners?|beacons?|collect|collectors?|counters?|crashlytics|dtrace|events|footprints|insights-collector|logging|marketing|metrics|pixels?|stats|statistics|telemetry|trace)[0-9]*(-[a-z0-9]+)*\.([a-z0-9-]+\.)+[a-z]{2,}$/

## Ad Server Keywords
### Same label rule for ad serving hosts: ad., ads1., adx-eu., adserver., adservice., adsystem., mads.

/^([a-z0-9-]+\.)*([a-z0-9]+-)?(ad|adbanners|adserv|adserver|adservers|adservice|adservices|ads|adsystem|adv|adx|mads)[0-9]*(-[a-z0-9]+)*\.([a-z0-9-]+\.)+[a-z]{2,}$/

## High-Abuse TLDs
### Highest spam scores per 10,000 registrations, Cybercrime Information Center (March to May 2026)
### and the most phished TLDs in Interisle's Phishing Landscape 2025, plus .zip and .mov
### Url: https://www.cybercrimeinfocenter.org/spam-activity-in-tlds-march-may-2026

/^([a-z0-9-]+\.)+(bid|bond|cam|cfd|click|help|icu|loan|mom|mov|qpon|rest|sbs|top|vip|win|xin|zip)$/
